## Evidence-bounded novelty statement

The sources reviewed establish prior work on elevator-aware MAPF, wait adjustment, hierarchical MAPF, industrial hierarchical traffic management, and distributed AGV traffic control. Within this targeted set, the specific combination evaluated by this project—prioritized job assignment, centralized lift booking, per-floor leg planning, and explicit lift-lobby waiting points—was not identified in the inspected papers. That observation is limited to the sources and accessible records listed above; it is not a claim of being the first such system. The closest overlaps are the elevator-aware MAPF formulations of He et al. and Werner, and the hierarchical industrial traffic management of Pratissoli et al.; their reported formulations differ from this project's combination of job scheduling, floor-local leg planning, and a central lift arbiter.
